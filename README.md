# Fabulous Beasts
A Chinese mythology inspired content expansion mod for Balatro. Based on [Fabulous Beasts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GY3jg2heY30) (the manga and anime)

## Current Version
Current version: `0.1.0-alpha`
This is an alpha release.
This version added:
* 5 unique challenges
* color coded placeholder art
* legendary jokers have their names in joker sprites (just the name)
* boss blinds, 30 to be exact; based on the Chinese zodiacs, heavenly stems, and wuxing (5 elements) + air, all with unique or expanded vanilla mechanics!
* a bunch of bug and crash fixes involving logic errors and nil/table handling
* many joker reworks and balances
* maybe some secrets

## Next Planned Update
`0.2.0-alpha` will be the next update if no additional patches are required. This update will add vouchers, booster packs, decks and/or sleeves (maybe), and some more challenges!

## Installation
1. Install Steamodded and Lovely Injector
2. Place the mod folder into the Balatro Mods folder
3. Launch the game

## FAQ
### Is there a Discord server?
Not yet. There will likely be a Discord server for the mod in the future

### Is Talisman required?
Yes. There are features in this mod that require Talisman

### Will there be localization?
Yes, but currently only Simplified and Traditional Chinese are planned.
If you would like to help with localization, feel free to DM me on Discord.

### What's your Discord username?
Keep reading. It's somewhere in here :)

### Why are some sprites placeholders?
The mod is currently in alpha and many assets are temporary

### Why did my game crash?
This is an alpha build. Expect bugs and crashes, and please report them if you encounter any

### How do I unlock Exotic and Divine Jokers?
Most Exotic and Divine Jokers cannot naturally appear and are obtained through secret unlocks and hidden interactions

### Why can’t I find certain Jokers?
Some Jokers are intentionally hidden or unavailable through normal gameplay

### What does Vision do?
Vision reveals whether a Joker has hidden interactions

### What does True Sight do?
True Sight reveals the exact hidden interactions between Jokers

### Are extremely large score values intended?
Yes

### Why did my score become unreadable?
Some Jokers are intentionally designed to scale to absurd values, especially with Talisman installed

### Why did Mortal Realm sell my Joker?
Because it wanted somewhere to live and is likely an asshole

### Why did Mortal Realm become Eternal?
Mortal Realm becomes Eternal after selling another Joker so it can be even more of an asshole

### Can I remove Eternal from Jokers?
Certain hidden Jokers and interactions can remove Eternal

### Why did my run instantly end?
Some hidden interactions and effects are intentionally destructive

### Are there hidden interactions?
Yes. Many Jokers contain hidden interactions and synergies

### Are there secret achievements?
Yes. They are still under construction and will likely be added during the beta release

### Is every interaction documented?
No. They are called secret interactions for a reason

### Can I suggest ideas?
Yes. Suggestions and feedback are welcome. Ideas related to:
* Chinese internet culture
* mythology
* references from the manga or animation
* existing themes in the mod
are much more likely to be considered

### Why doesn't Album Cover and Alternate Album Cover work?
If you're talking about the music changing aspect, I haven't figured it out yet, but it will work eventually! As for the effects with another joker, it should work properly

### What other references are there?
That's for you to figure out. The biggest reference is that this mod is based on Fabulous Beasts (manga and animated series) and the blinds are based on Chinese zodiacs, stems, Wuxing (5 elements), and some Chinese internet culture

### Are artists wanted?
Yes. The mod currently uses placeholder assets for many Jokers

### Can I help with mod development?
Absolutely! Any help is welcome! Just send me a DM on Discord or a pull request!

### When will the mod be out of alpha?
Once most of the art is complete and the mod is free of major bugs, glitches, and unexpected crashes
